# Extraction card — optional

Extract only what the user's requested output needs:

- the source's own claim, method, setting, controls, and limitations;
- numbers with the table, figure, page, or log location;
- evidence depth (abstract, methods, results, or full source); abstract-only evidence may
  support an attributed, softened high-level direction or conclusion, but not numeric results,
  comparisons, baselines, metrics, or detailed conditions; and
- code, data, version, and reproducibility notes when relevant.

Read and record the named page, table, figure, log, or section before quoting or repeating a
number. An abstract-only record can support the authors' attributed, softened high-level claim,
not a value such as 4 points or 20%; a results read with a locator can support the value directly
(see [06-hedge](06-hedge.md)). Use `not reported` for information the source does not provide.
Keep notes small and keyed to stable identifiers. A corpus can hold partial records; missing
extraction is a limitation, not a workflow failure.
