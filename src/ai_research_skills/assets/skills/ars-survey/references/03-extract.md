# Extraction card — optional

Extract only what the user's requested output needs:

- the source's own claim, method, setting, controls, and limitations;
- numbers with the table, figure, page, or log location;
- evidence depth (abstract, methods, results, or full source); and
- code, data, version, and reproducibility notes when relevant.

Read the original location before quoting a number. Use `not reported` for information the
source does not provide. Keep notes small and keyed to stable identifiers. A corpus can hold
partial records; missing extraction is a limitation, not a workflow failure.
