## Why this pair matters
Reports the opposite direction to alpha2025iterative on an overlapping benchmark. Neither
controls retrieval recall, so this is not a contradiction — it is two experiments that
cannot be compared, which is a stronger motivation for the gap than either result alone.

Do not smooth this into "several works have explored…" when drafting. The disagreement is
the interesting sentence.

## Reproducibility
Official repo exists but has no evaluation script for the multi-hop setting; the reported
numbers could not be reproduced. `runs: unverified`.
